What can I say?

Say a number to enter it. You may also use an exact answer phrase such as “the answer is forty-two”; equivalent French, German, and Vietnamese phrases are supported. Say “check” or “done” when ready. If immediate checking is enabled, a correct answer advances automatically; after the tenth answer, the set completes. “Next,” “go on,” and their language equivalents work only after the current answer is correct. Say “stop” to turn voice input off.

The studio requires an on-device language pack and never falls back to an online speech service. A successful transcription can support language practice, but it is not a standardized pronunciation score.

    Where this can grow

    Arithmetic is one doorway

    The studio is designed for many forms of elementary mathematics. New activities should remain visual, explainable, culturally adaptable, and genuinely original.

    Patterns & puzzles

    Notice structure, predict what comes next, and explain why a pattern works.

    Shape & space

    Build, fold, rotate, and reason with diagrams before reaching for formulas.

    Stories & choices

    Use estimation and number sense in questions with more than one good path.

    Explain your thinking

    Make room for drawings, words, symbols, and learner-created examples.

    The studio happens here

    A microphone without a listening service

    The repository is the maker and governance layer. What it produces is the studio: a learner-owned experience running on the learner’s own device.

    1

    You choose when to listen

    Voice starts only after a deliberate button press and the browser’s microphone permission. It stops when asked, when the page is hidden, or when a set ends.

    2

    The browser proves it is local

    The studio requires the standardized local-processing flag and checks for an installed on-device language pack. If either check fails, voice stays off.

    3

    No silent cloud fallback

    Microphone audio is processed by the browser on this device. The studio receives recognized text, does not keep audio, and does not send either one to a server.

    Firefox Nightly and current desktop Chrome can provide this local contract.

    Support is detected by capability, not by browser brand. Mobile browser support is not ready yet.
