# Family guide

These sheets are short practice tools, not tests of mathematical worth. The goal is to notice patterns, choose strategies, and become more accurate and efficient over time.

## A useful 10-minute routine

1. **Preview (1 minute).** Pick one strategy to try. Examples: doubles, make 10, skip-count, use a known 5s fact, or reverse a multiplication fact for division.
2. **Practice (5-7 minutes).** Work comfortably. Use a timer only when the learner wants to measure their own progress.
3. **Check (1 minute).** An adult may check the work or discuss a few uncertain facts. Do not write answers on the reusable master.
4. **Reflect (1 minute).** Record one strategy that helped and one small next focus.

## Timing modes

- **Untimed:** best for learning a new strategy or rebuilding confidence.
- **Optional timing:** record a time only if it feels useful. Accuracy comes first.
- **Personal challenge:** repeat the same range later and compare with the learner's own previous attempt.

Stop the timer if it creates distress, guessing, freezing, or conflict. A smaller set completed thoughtfully is more useful than 100 rushed guesses.

## Interpreting progress

Use the CSV log to look for trends across several sessions:

- accuracy is stable or improving;
- more questions are attempted without more errors;
- the learner can explain a strategy;
- confidence is improving;
- a difficult fact family becomes easier.

Do not turn one session into a grade. Sleep, attention, familiarity, and mood all affect speed.

## Suggested Grade 4 focus

- Keep addition and subtraction facts through 20 flexible and reliable.
- Develop multiplication and division facts within 100.
- Give extra attention to fluent recall of 2s, 5s, and 10s.
- Connect facts to arrays, groups, skip-counting, and fact families.
- Mix fluency pages with puzzles, games, estimation, word problems, and explanations.

## Printing

Print at 100% scale on US Letter paper. The PDF is black-and-white friendly; colour is decorative and not required to understand the worksheet.
